Why do some voices only work online?

Speech synthesis engines fall into two categories: Local Offline (synthesized on your device processor using built-in OS packages) and Cloud Neural Online (processed on server neural networks for superior natural cadence). Online voices require an active internet connection because the audio stream is streamed dynamically from cloud TTS providers.
